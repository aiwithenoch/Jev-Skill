# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to making Jev Skill a welcoming, respectful, and practical
open-source project for people building reliable AI systems. Participate with
curiosity, assume good intent, explain disagreements clearly, and welcome
contributors at every experience level.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and publishing private information
without permission.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainers through the contact options on
<https://aiwithenoch.com>. Please do not post sensitive details in a public
issue.
